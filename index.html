游戏玩法HOW TO PLAY

  • 灯灭起步。W / 右侧油门，S / 左侧刹车，可同时踩。

    Wait for lights out. W / right: throttle. S / left: brake. Both work together.

  • 保持在 250 km/h 以内，超速会爆缸。

    Stay at or below 250 km/h. Overspeed blows the engine.

  • 尽快冲过 1250 米终点，冲线后动力切断。

    Cross the 1,250 m finish fast. Power cuts at the line.

  • 停在 250 米外的路障前，越近越好。刹车压力到 115% 会打滑，松开卸压。

    Stop close to the barrier 250 m beyond. Release the brake before pressure reaches 115% to avoid locking up.
